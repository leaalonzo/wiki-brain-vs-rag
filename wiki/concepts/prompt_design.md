## Definition
Prompt design refers to the process of crafting input prompts to guide large language models (LLMs) in generating desired outputs. It is a critical aspect of interacting with LLMs, influencing their performance and the quality of the generated content.

## Context
Prompt design plays a vital role in the effectiveness of Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting, a strategy that enhances the reasoning capabilities of LLMs by guiding them through step-wise reasoning processes. CoT prompting is particularly useful for multi-step reasoning tasks, as it incorporates intermediate steps to improve interpretability and ensure a logical and traceable reasoning process. The effectiveness of CoT prompting is influenced by several factors, including task types, prompt design, extension strategies, and the models used.

Despite its advantages, CoT prompting faces challenges such as generality, opaque mechanisms, and unfaithful generation. Addressing these challenges is crucial for improving the reliability and applicability of CoT prompting in various tasks.
