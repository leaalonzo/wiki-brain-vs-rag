## Definition
Multilingual instruction tuning is a process applied to large language models (LLMs) to enhance their performance across multiple languages. This process involves refining the models using instruction and response-ranked data in various languages, often leveraging reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).

## Context
The concept of multilingual instruction tuning has gained prominence with the development of systems like Okapi, which aim to overcome the limitations of existing LLMs that predominantly focus on English and a few other languages. By incorporating RLHF, Okapi introduces instruction tuning in 26 diverse languages, demonstrating improved accessibility and performance of multilingual LLMs. This approach contrasts with traditional supervised fine-tuning (SFT), showcasing the benefits of RLHF in multilingual contexts.
