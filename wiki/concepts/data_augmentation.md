## Definition
Data augmentation is a technique used in machine learning to increase the diversity of training data without collecting new data. It involves creating modified versions of existing data points to improve the robustness and performance of models.

## Context
In machine learning and natural language processing, data augmentation is employed to enhance model training by artificially expanding the dataset. This is particularly useful in scenarios with limited labeled data, as it helps improve the model's ability to generalize. RocketQA, an optimized training approach for dense passage retrieval in open-domain question answering systems, utilizes data augmentation alongside other strategies like cross-batch negatives and denoised hard negatives. These strategies address challenges such as the discrepancy between training and inference, and the presence of unlabeled positives, significantly improving performance on datasets like MS-MARCO and Natural Questions. RocketQA's approach has been shown to outperform previous state-of-the-art models, demonstrating the effectiveness of data augmentation in enhancing retrieval performance.
