## Definition
Prompting strategies refer to techniques used to guide large language models (LLMs) in generating desired outputs. These strategies enhance the performance of LLMs by structuring the input prompts to align with task requirements and leverage the model's capabilities.

## Context
Prompting strategies play a crucial role in optimizing the performance of LLMs, particularly in tasks requiring complex reasoning and human-like understanding. One prominent strategy is Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting, which improves multi-step reasoning by guiding the model through a step-wise reasoning process. This approach enhances interpretability by ensuring logical and traceable outputs. The effectiveness of CoT prompting is influenced by factors such as task types, prompt design, extension strategies, and the models used. Despite its advantages, CoT prompting faces challenges related to generality, opaque mechanisms, and unfaithful generation, highlighting the need for continued research and development.
