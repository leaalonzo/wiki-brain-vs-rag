## Definition
A reranker model is a component in information retrieval systems designed to improve the accuracy of document retrieval by reordering the results obtained from an initial retrieval process. It typically uses advanced machine learning techniques to evaluate and rank the relevance of documents more precisely than the initial retrieval model.

## Context
Reranker models are crucial in specialized fields where high retrieval accuracy is necessary, such as electronic design automation (EDA). In these domains, general-purpose retrieval systems may not suffice due to the complexity and specificity of the information. The paper "Customized Retrieval Augmented Generation and Benchmarking for EDA Tool Documentation QA" highlights the use of a reranker model distilled from proprietary large language models (LLMs) to enhance document retrieval accuracy in a customized retrieval augmented generation (RAG) framework. This approach is part of a broader effort to tailor retrieval systems to domain-specific needs, thereby improving the quality of question-answering systems in knowledge-intensive areas.
