## Definition
Reasoning steps refer to the individual components or stages involved in the process of deriving a conclusion or solving a problem. In the context of artificial intelligence, particularly large language models (LLMs), reasoning steps are the intermediate rationales generated during multi-step reasoning tasks.

## Context
The concept of reasoning steps is central to the study of Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting, a technique used to enhance the reasoning capabilities of LLMs. CoT prompting involves guiding LLMs to produce a series of intermediate steps that lead to a final answer, thereby improving their ability to handle complex queries. Research indicates that while the validity of each reasoning step is not critical—invalid steps can still achieve a significant portion of CoT's performance—the relevance and correct ordering of these steps are crucial for effective reasoning.

The study "Towards Understanding Chain-of-Thought Prompting: An Empirical Study of What Matters" highlights that LLMs may have inherent reasoning abilities developed during pretraining. CoT prompting primarily influences the format of the output rather than the reasoning ability itself.
