## Definition
In-depth dialogue questions refer to queries within a conversation that require comprehensive and thoughtful responses. These questions often delve into complex topics, necessitating a deeper level of reasoning and understanding from dialogue systems.

## Context
The concept of in-depth dialogue questions is particularly relevant in the development of advanced dialogue systems, such as those utilizing Large Language Models (LLMs). The paper "Cue-CoT: Chain-of-thought Prompting for Responding to In-depth Dialogue Questions with LLMs" introduces a method called Cue-CoT, which enhances the ability of LLMs to handle such questions. Cue-CoT incorporates intermediate reasoning steps to identify linguistic cues like personality, emotion, and psychology, thereby generating more personalized and engaging responses.
