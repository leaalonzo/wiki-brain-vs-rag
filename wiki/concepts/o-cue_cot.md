## Definition
O-Cue CoT is a variant of the Cue-CoT method, a chain-of-thought prompting technique designed to enhance dialogue systems using Large Language Models (LLMs). It focuses on incorporating intermediate reasoning steps to identify linguistic cues such as personality, emotion, and psychology within dialogues.

## Context
O-Cue CoT is part of a broader approach introduced in the paper "Cue-CoT: Chain-of-thought Prompting for Responding to In-depth Dialogue Questions with LLMs." This method aims to generate more personalized and engaging responses by recognizing and utilizing linguistic cues. The approach has been evaluated using a benchmark of six datasets in both Chinese and English, demonstrating improvements in helpfulness and acceptability over standard prompting methods. Although O-Cue CoT is one of the proposed variants, the M-Cue CoT variant has shown superior robustness and reasoning performance. Both variants outperform standard prompting methods in generating more helpful and acceptable responses.
