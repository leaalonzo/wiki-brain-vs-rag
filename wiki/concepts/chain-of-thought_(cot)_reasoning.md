## Definition
Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning is a method used in artificial intelligence, particularly in large language models (LLMs), to enhance the reasoning process by breaking down complex questions into a series of intermediate reasoning steps. This approach helps in improving the accuracy and factual correctness of multi-step question answering.

## Context
CoT reasoning is particularly useful in scenarios where questions require multiple steps to arrive at a correct answer. Traditional one-step retrieval methods often fall short in such cases due to their inability to handle complex reasoning tasks effectively. The introduction of methods like IRCoT, which interleaves retrieval with CoT reasoning, has shown significant improvements in both retrieval accuracy and reasoning performance. This approach has been tested on datasets such as HotpotQA, 2WikiMultihopQA, MuSiQue, and IIRC, demonstrating substantial improvements in retrieval and question answering performance.
