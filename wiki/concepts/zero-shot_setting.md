## Definition
A zero-shot setting refers to a scenario in machine learning and artificial intelligence where a model is required to make predictions or perform tasks without having been explicitly trained on any examples of the specific task or category. Instead, the model leverages its general understanding and pre-existing knowledge to infer and respond to new, unseen situations.

## Context
In the context of dialogue systems and Large Language Models (LLMs), a zero-shot setting is particularly challenging because the model must generate appropriate responses without prior exposure to specific dialogue examples. The paper "Cue-CoT: Chain-of-thought Prompting for Responding to In-depth Dialogue Questions with LLMs" explores this setting by introducing Cue-CoT, a method that enhances response generation by identifying linguistic cues such as personality, emotion, and psychology. This approach allows the model to provide more personalized and engaging responses even in zero-shot scenarios.
