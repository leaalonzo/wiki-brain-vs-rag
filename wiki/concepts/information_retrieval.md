## Definition
Information Retrieval (IR) is the process of obtaining relevant information from a large repository, such as a database or the internet, based on a user's query. It involves the indexing, searching, and ranking of documents to provide the most pertinent results to the user.

## Context
Information Retrieval is a fundamental aspect of various fields, including search engines, digital libraries, and question-answering systems. In specialized domains, such as electronic design automation (EDA), standard IR systems may face challenges due to a lack of domain-specific knowledge. The paper "Customized Retrieval Augmented Generation and Benchmarking for EDA Tool Documentation QA" addresses these challenges by proposing a customized Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) framework tailored for EDA tool documentation question-answering (QA). This framework incorporates domain-specific techniques like contrastive learning for text embedding and fine-tuning large language models (LLMs) with EDA-specific corpora. Additionally, the ORD-QA benchmark is introduced to evaluate the performance of these systems, demonstrating superior results compared to state-of-the-art methods.
