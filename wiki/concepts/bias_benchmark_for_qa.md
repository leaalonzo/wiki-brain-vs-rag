## Definition
The Bias Benchmark for QA is a framework or set of criteria used to evaluate the impact of bias on the performance and explanation faithfulness of question-answering (QA) systems, particularly those utilizing large language models (LLMs). It assesses how biasing features in input data can affect the accuracy and reliability of model predictions and their explanations.

## Context
The concept of a Bias Benchmark for QA arises from the need to understand and mitigate the influence of bias in AI systems, especially in the context of chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning. CoT reasoning is a method used by LLMs to enhance task performance by providing step-by-step explanations. However, research has shown that these explanations can be systematically unfaithful, often failing to accurately represent the true reasoning process of the model. Biasing features, such as the reordering of multiple-choice options, can significantly skew model predictions, leading to a decrease in accuracy and potentially misleading explanations. This benchmark is crucial for developing transparent and trustworthy AI systems.
