## Definition
FreshQA is a dynamic question-answering (QA) benchmark designed to evaluate the factual accuracy of text generated by large language models (LLMs) in response to questions that require up-to-date world knowledge and the ability to debunk false premises.

## Context
FreshQA was developed as part of the study "FreshLLMs: Refreshing Large Language Models with Search Engine Augmentation." It addresses the limitations of LLMs in adapting to rapidly changing information. The benchmark consists of 600 questions categorized into never-changing, slow-changing, fast-changing, and false-premise types. It evaluates LLMs on correctness and hallucination through two evaluation modes: RELAXED and STRICT. Human evaluations with over 50,000 judgments indicate that LLMs struggle with fast-changing knowledge and false-premise questions, regardless of model size.

FreshPrompt, a few-shot prompting method introduced in the study, significantly enhances LLM performance by incorporating real-time data from search engines. This method outperforms other search-augmented methods and commercial systems like PERPLEXITY.AI, demonstrating improvements of up to 49.0% in accuracy over traditional LLM methods. The study highlights the importance of the order and number of retrieved evidences in generating correct answers and notes that concise responses can reduce hallucinations in LLM outputs. FreshQA is available on GitHub and is regularly updated to support ongoing research.
