## Definition
Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting is a strategy used in large language models (LLMs) to enhance their reasoning capabilities by guiding them through a step-wise reasoning process. This approach aims to improve multi-step reasoning and ensure a logical and interpretable reasoning chain, which is beneficial for human understanding.

## Context
Chain-of-Thought prompting is explored in the survey "Towards Better Chain-of-Thought Prompting Strategies: A Survey." The paper provides a comprehensive analysis of CoT prompting, discussing its effectiveness across various tasks and identifying challenges and future research directions. Key factors influencing CoT prompting include task types, prompt design, extension strategies, and models. While CoT prompting improves LLM performance and interpretability, it faces challenges related to generality, opaque mechanisms, and unfaithful generation.
