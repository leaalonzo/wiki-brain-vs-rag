## Definition
Reasoning on Large Language Models (LLM) refers to the process by which these models perform logical and step-wise analysis to arrive at conclusions or solve problems. This involves leveraging advanced prompting strategies, such as Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting, to enhance the model's ability to handle complex, multi-step reasoning tasks.

## Context
The concept of reasoning on LLMs is significantly enhanced by the Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting strategy. CoT prompting guides LLMs through a coherent, step-wise reasoning process, which improves their performance on tasks requiring multi-step logic. This strategy is particularly beneficial for ensuring that the reasoning process is logical and interpretable, aiding human understanding. Key factors influencing the effectiveness of CoT prompting include task types, prompt design, extension strategies, and the models themselves. Despite its advantages, CoT prompting faces challenges related to generality, opaque mechanisms, and unfaithful generation, which are areas for future research.
