## Definition
Task types refer to the various categories or kinds of tasks that can be addressed or solved using specific strategies or methodologies. In the context of large language models (LLMs) and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting, task types are crucial as they influence the effectiveness of the reasoning processes employed by these models.

## Context
In the survey "Towards Better Chain-of-Thought Prompting Strategies: A Survey," task types are identified as one of the four key factors affecting the performance of CoT prompting. CoT prompting is a strategy that enhances the reasoning capabilities of LLMs by guiding them through step-wise reasoning processes, making it particularly effective for multi-step reasoning tasks. The choice of task type can significantly impact how well CoT prompting strategies work, as different tasks may require different approaches to reasoning and problem-solving.
