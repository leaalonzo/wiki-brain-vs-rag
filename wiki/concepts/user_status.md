## Definition
User status refers to the dynamic indicators of a user's characteristics and conditions, such as personality, emotion, and psychology, which can influence interactions in dialogue systems. These indicators are crucial for personalizing and enhancing the engagement of responses generated by dialogue systems.

## Context
In the context of dialogue systems, understanding user status is essential for tailoring responses that are more relevant and engaging. The paper "Cue-CoT: Chain-of-thought Prompting for Responding to In-depth Dialogue Questions with LLMs" highlights the importance of user status by introducing a Cue-CoT methodology. This approach uses linguistic cues to improve the inference capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs), thereby enhancing response personalization by considering user status indicators.
