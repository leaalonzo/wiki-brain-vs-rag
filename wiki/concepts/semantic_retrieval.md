## Definition
Semantic Retrieval is an advanced information retrieval technique that focuses on understanding the meaning and context of words within a document to improve the accuracy and relevance of search results. Unlike traditional lexical retrieval methods, which rely on keyword matching, semantic retrieval uses natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning techniques to interpret the intent behind queries and the content of documents.

## Context
Semantic retrieval is particularly useful in specialized fields where domain-specific knowledge is crucial for accurate information retrieval. In the paper "Customized Retrieval Augmented Generation and Benchmarking for EDA Tool Documentation QA," the authors address the challenges faced by retrieval augmented generation (RAG) systems in the electronic design automation (EDA) domain. They propose a customized RAG framework that incorporates semantic retrieval techniques, such as contrastive learning and domain-specific fine-tuning, to enhance the accuracy of question-answering systems for EDA tool documentation. The study also introduces the ORD-QA benchmark to evaluate the effectiveness of these techniques in improving retrieval and QA quality.
