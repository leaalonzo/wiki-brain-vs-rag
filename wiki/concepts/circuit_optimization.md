## Definition
Circuit optimization refers to the process of improving the performance, efficiency, and resource utilization of electronic circuits. This can involve reducing the area, power consumption, and clock period of the hardware design, often through techniques such as bit width optimization and program repair.

## Context
Circuit optimization is a critical aspect of electronic design automation (EDA) and is essential for enhancing the performance of integrated circuits (ICs) and systems on chips (SoCs). The process can be manual or automated, with recent advancements leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) to automate the conversion of C/C++ programs into High-Level Synthesis-compatible (HLS-C) code. This automation reduces the need for human intervention and improves the efficiency of the design process. The use of LLMs, combined with techniques like Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), has shown significant improvements in reducing area, power, and clock period in hardware design.
