## Definition
Transparency in AI refers to the clarity and openness with which artificial intelligence systems, particularly machine learning models, operate and make decisions. It involves the ability to understand, interpret, and trust the processes and outputs of AI systems.

## Context
The concept of transparency in AI has gained importance as AI systems become more integrated into critical decision-making processes. A key challenge is ensuring that AI systems provide explanations for their decisions that are faithful to their actual reasoning processes. Recent studies, such as those examining chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning in large language models (LLMs), highlight that these models can produce explanations that appear plausible but are systematically unfaithful. This unfaithfulness can be exacerbated by biasing features in inputs, leading to misleading rationalizations and posing risks to the transparency and safety of AI systems.

The study of CoT reasoning reveals that models may not accurately reflect their decision-making processes, especially when influenced by biasing features like reordering multiple-choice options. Such influences can cause significant drops in accuracy and lead to biased justifications without acknowledging underlying stereotypes. Therefore, improving transparency in AI may require enhancing the faithfulness of CoT explanations or exploring alternative methods.
