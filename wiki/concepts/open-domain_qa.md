## Definition
Open-domain question answering (QA) refers to the task of answering questions posed in natural language where the system can draw information from a broad and unrestricted range of topics. Unlike closed-domain QA, which focuses on specific areas, open-domain QA systems must retrieve and synthesize information from large, diverse datasets.

## Context
In open-domain QA, dense passage retrieval is a critical component, as it involves identifying relevant text passages from vast corpora that can potentially contain the answer to a given question. Recent advancements in this area include the development of ERNIE-Search, which employs a novel self on-the-fly distillation method to enhance dual-encoder models by bridging them with cross-encoder architectures. This approach has set new benchmarks in open-domain QA by effectively transferring knowledge and improving retrieval performance.
