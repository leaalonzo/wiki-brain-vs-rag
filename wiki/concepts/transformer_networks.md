## Definition
Transformer Networks are a type of deep learning model architecture primarily used for natural language processing tasks. They utilize self-attention mechanisms to process and generate sequences of data, allowing for parallelization and improved handling of long-range dependencies compared to previous models like recurrent neural networks.

## Context
Transformer Networks have become foundational in the development of advanced language models and systems, such as BERT and GPT. They are particularly effective in tasks requiring understanding and generation of human language, including translation, summarization, and question-answering systems. Recent advancements have explored integrating retrieval mechanisms within transformer layers to enhance performance. The FetcHR method, for example, leverages hierarchical representations for improved dense passage retrieval, demonstrating state-of-the-art performance on datasets like Natural Question and WebQuestion. FetcHR integrates retrieval at each layer of a transformer network, enhancing the retrieval process and ultimately improving the performance of downstream tasks such as question answering.
