## Definition
Interpretability refers to the degree to which a human can understand the cause of a decision made by a model or system. In the context of machine learning and artificial intelligence, it involves the ability to comprehend the reasoning process and the outputs of models, ensuring that they are logical and traceable.

## Context
Interpretability is a crucial aspect of artificial intelligence and machine learning, particularly when dealing with complex models like large language models (LLMs). Techniques such as Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting enhance interpretability by guiding models through step-wise reasoning processes. This approach allows for a more transparent understanding of how models arrive at their conclusions, which is essential for validating their outputs and ensuring they align with human reasoning.

Despite its advantages, achieving interpretability in AI systems can be challenging due to the complexity and opacity of the models. CoT prompting, while beneficial, faces issues such as generality and unfaithful generation, which can hinder the interpretability of the reasoning process.
