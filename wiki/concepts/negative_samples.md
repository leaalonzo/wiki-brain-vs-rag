## Definition
Negative samples are data points used in machine learning models, particularly in contrastive learning, that do not match the target output. They are used to help the model learn to differentiate between correct and incorrect matches by providing examples of what the model should not predict.

## Context
In the context of contrastive learning and dual-encoder models, negative samples play a crucial role in training models to distinguish between similar inputs. For instance, in open-domain question answering systems like xMoCo (Cross Momentum Contrastive Learning), negative samples are used to improve the model's ability to retrieve relevant passages by contrasting them with irrelevant ones. Efficient management of negative samples is essential for enhancing the performance of dense passage retrieval models, which encode questions and passages into vector representations for effective retrieval.
