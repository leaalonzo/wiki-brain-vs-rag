## Definition
Large language models (LLMs) are advanced artificial intelligence systems designed to understand, generate, and manipulate human language. They are built using deep learning techniques and are trained on vast amounts of text data to perform a wide range of language-related tasks.

## Context
LLMs have gained prominence due to their ability to perform complex language tasks such as translation, summarization, and question-answering with high accuracy. A significant development in enhancing their reasoning capabilities is the Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting strategy. CoT prompting guides LLMs through a step-wise reasoning process, improving their ability to handle multi-step reasoning tasks. This strategy enhances interpretability by ensuring a logical and traceable reasoning process, which is crucial for human understanding. Despite its advantages, CoT prompting faces challenges related to generality, opaque mechanisms, and unfaithful generation. Future research directions aim to address these challenges and further refine CoT prompting strategies.
