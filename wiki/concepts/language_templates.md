## Definition
Language templates refer to structured formats or patterns utilized in natural language processing to guide the generation or interpretation of text. These templates provide a framework for organizing information or reasoning through a problem, thereby enhancing the performance of language models.

## Context
In the realm of large language models (LLMs), language templates are pivotal in enhancing reasoning capabilities. They are particularly significant in Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting, a technique that improves multi-step reasoning by structuring reasoning steps through intermediate rationales. The study "Towards Understanding Chain-of-Thought Prompting: An Empirical Study of What Matters" highlights that while the validity of reasoning steps is less critical, the relevance and correct ordering of these steps are essential for effective CoT reasoning. This indicates that language templates help guide the output format, leveraging the inherent reasoning abilities that LLMs may possess from pretraining.
