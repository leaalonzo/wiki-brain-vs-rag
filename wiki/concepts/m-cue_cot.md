## Definition
M-Cue CoT is a variant of the Cue-CoT (Chain-of-thought) prompting method designed to enhance dialogue systems using Large Language Models (LLMs). It focuses on incorporating intermediate reasoning steps to identify linguistic cues such as personality, emotion, and psychology within dialogues, aiming to generate more personalized and engaging responses.

## Context
M-Cue CoT is part of a broader approach introduced in the paper "Cue-CoT: Chain-of-thought Prompting for Responding to In-depth Dialogue Questions with LLMs." This method enhances the inference capabilities of LLMs in dialogue systems by using a linguistic cue-based chain-of-thought methodology. It aims to improve response personalization and engagement by considering user status indicators. M-Cue CoT, along with its counterpart O-Cue CoT, was evaluated using a benchmark of six datasets in both Chinese and English. Empirical results demonstrated that M-Cue CoT outperformed standard prompting methods in terms of helpfulness and acceptability, showing superior robustness and reasoning performance compared to O-Cue CoT.
