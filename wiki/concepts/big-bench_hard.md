## Definition
BIG-Bench Hard is a collection of challenging tasks designed to evaluate the performance of large language models (LLMs) on complex and nuanced problems. It is a subset of the broader BIG-Bench benchmark, focusing specifically on tasks that are difficult for models to solve, often requiring advanced reasoning and understanding.

## Context
The BIG-Bench Hard tasks are used to assess the capabilities of LLMs in scenarios that demand more than simple pattern recognition or memorization. These tasks are particularly useful for identifying the limitations of models, especially when it comes to reasoning and explanation generation. The paper "Language Models Don’t Always Say What They Think: Unfaithful Explanations in Chain-of-Thought Prompting" highlights the challenges models face with BIG-Bench Hard tasks, noting a significant drop in accuracy when models are exposed to biasing features. This underscores the importance of developing more reliable and faithful explanation techniques, such as chain-of-thought reasoning, to ensure transparency and trust in AI systems.
