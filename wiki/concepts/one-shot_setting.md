## Definition
A one-shot setting in the context of machine learning and artificial intelligence refers to a scenario where a model is trained or prompted to perform a task based on a single example or instance. This setting contrasts with zero-shot settings, where no examples are provided, and few-shot settings, where a small number of examples are used.

## Context
In dialogue systems and natural language processing, one-shot settings are particularly challenging because the model must generalize from minimal data. The "Cue-CoT: Chain-of-thought Prompting for Responding to In-depth Dialogue Questions with LLMs" paper discusses the use of one-shot settings as part of its exploration of enhancing dialogue systems with Large Language Models (LLMs). The paper introduces Cue-CoT, a method that leverages linguistic cues to improve response generation, even in limited data scenarios like one-shot settings.
