## Definition
Electronic Design Automation (EDA) refers to the category of software tools used for designing electronic systems such as integrated circuits and printed circuit boards. These tools assist engineers in the design, analysis, and verification of complex electronic systems, enhancing productivity and accuracy.

## Context
EDA tools are crucial in the semiconductor industry, enabling the design and production of increasingly complex electronic components. The field has seen significant advancements with the integration of artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to improve design efficiency and accuracy. A notable development is the application of customized Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) systems for EDA tool documentation question-answering (QA). The paper "Customized Retrieval Augmented Generation and Benchmarking for EDA Tool Documentation QA" highlights the limitations of standard RAG systems in EDA due to the need for domain-specific knowledge and proposes enhancements to improve retrieval accuracy and QA quality. The study introduces a customized RAG framework tailored for EDA, incorporating a contrastive learning scheme, a reranker model, and a generative LLM fine-tuned with EDA-specific knowledge. Additionally, the ORD-QA benchmark is developed to evaluate the proposed RAG flow, demonstrating superior performance on both academic and commercial EDA tools.
