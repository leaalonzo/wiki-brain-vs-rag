## Definition
Cue-CoT, or Chain-of-thought Prompting for Responding to In-depth Dialogue Questions, is a method designed to enhance dialogue systems using Large Language Models (LLMs). It incorporates intermediate reasoning steps to identify linguistic cues such as personality, emotion, and psychology within dialogues, thereby generating more personalized and engaging responses.

## Context
The Cue-CoT approach was introduced to improve the inference capabilities of LLMs in dialogue systems. By focusing on linguistic cues, Cue-CoT seeks to provide more nuanced and contextually appropriate responses in conversations. The method has been evaluated using a benchmark comprising six datasets in both Chinese and English, demonstrating superior performance in terms of helpfulness and acceptability compared to standard prompting methods.

Two variants of Cue-CoT have been proposed: O-Cue CoT and M-Cue CoT. Among these, M-Cue CoT has shown greater robustness and reasoning performance in handling dialogue tasks. The study also suggests using intermediate reasoning results for demonstration selection in scenarios with limited training data. Empirical results indicate that both variants outperform standard prompting methods, enhancing the generation of helpful and acceptable responses.
