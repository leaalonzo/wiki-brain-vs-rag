## Definition
Lexical Retrieval refers to the process of retrieving information based on the exact matching of words or phrases from a query with those in a dataset or document collection. It relies on the lexical similarity between the query and the documents, often using techniques such as keyword matching or Boolean search.

## Context
In the context of information retrieval and question-answering systems, lexical retrieval serves as a foundational approach for identifying relevant documents or passages that contain specific terms from a user's query. While effective for straightforward queries, lexical retrieval can struggle with understanding the semantic meaning or context beyond the literal words used. This limitation is particularly evident in specialized fields like electronic design automation (EDA), where domain-specific knowledge is crucial for accurate information retrieval.

The paper "Customized Retrieval Augmented Generation and Benchmarking for EDA Tool Documentation QA" highlights the challenges of applying standard retrieval augmented generation (RAG) systems in the EDA domain. It suggests enhancements to improve retrieval accuracy, including the integration of lexical and semantic retrieval techniques. The study introduces a customized RAG framework and a new QA evaluation benchmark, ORD-QA, to address these challenges.
