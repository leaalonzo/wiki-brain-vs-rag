## Definition
A probabilistic framework is a mathematical structure used to model uncertainty and make inferences about random variables. It involves the use of probability theory to describe and analyze systems or processes that exhibit randomness.

## Context
In the paper "An Encoder Attribution Analysis for Dense Passage Retriever in Open-Domain Question Answering," a probabilistic framework called encoder marginalization is introduced. This framework is used to analyze the contributions of different components within Dense Passage Retriever (DPR) systems, specifically focusing on the question and passage encoders. By marginalizing other variables, the framework quantifies each encoder's contribution to retrieval accuracy, revealing that the passage encoder plays a more significant role than the question encoder. This approach allows for optimizing training data usage without compromising performance.
