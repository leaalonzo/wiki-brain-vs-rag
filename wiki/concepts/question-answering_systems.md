## Definition
Question-answering systems are artificial intelligence applications designed to automatically answer questions posed by humans in natural language. These systems typically involve the retrieval of relevant information from a large corpus of data and the generation of precise answers.

## Context
Recent advancements in question-answering systems have focused on improving the retrieval phase, which is critical for the overall performance of these systems. A novel approach, FetcHR, enhances dense passage retrieval by utilizing hierarchical representations within transformer networks. This method integrates retrieval into each layer of a transformer network, leveraging the different levels of abstraction to improve the quality of retrieved documents. FetcHR has shown to outperform traditional dense passage retrieval (DPR) methods by better addressing critical features in questions that are often underrepresented in retrieved documents. It has demonstrated improved performance on benchmarks such as the Natural Question and WebQuestion datasets.
