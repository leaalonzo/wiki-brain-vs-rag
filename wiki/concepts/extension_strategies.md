## Definition
Extension strategies refer to methods used to enhance the effectiveness of Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting in large language models (LLMs). These strategies involve refining and expanding the reasoning processes that guide LLMs through complex, multi-step tasks.

## Context
In the realm of artificial intelligence and natural language processing, Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting is a technique that improves the reasoning capabilities of LLMs by incorporating intermediate steps in problem-solving. Extension strategies are one of the four key factors identified as significantly impacting the performance of CoT prompting. By employing these strategies, researchers aim to enhance the interpretability and accuracy of LLMs in various applications. Despite their potential, extension strategies must address challenges such as maintaining generality, ensuring transparency in the reasoning process, and avoiding unfaithful generation of results.
