## Definition
Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) is a method used in information retrieval to combine the results of multiple ranked lists into a single, more accurate ranking. It assigns a score to each item based on its position in each list, with higher scores given to items appearing higher in the rankings. The scores are then aggregated to produce a final ranking that aims to improve retrieval performance by leveraging the strengths of different retrieval methods.

## Context
Reciprocal Rank Fusion is particularly useful in scenarios where multiple retrieval systems or algorithms are used, each providing a different perspective or focus. By combining these perspectives, RRF can enhance the overall retrieval accuracy and robustness. This method is often employed in domains requiring high precision and recall, such as electronic design automation (EDA), where the complexity and specificity of the information necessitate advanced retrieval techniques.
