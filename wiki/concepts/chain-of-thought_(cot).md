## Definition
Chain-of-Thought (CoT) is a reasoning approach used in large language models (LLMs) to enhance multi-step question answering (QA). It involves breaking down complex questions into a series of intermediate reasoning steps, allowing the model to process and answer questions more effectively.

## Context
Chain-of-Thought reasoning is particularly valuable in scenarios where questions require multiple steps to arrive at an answer. By structuring the reasoning process, CoT helps in reducing errors and improving the factual accuracy of the responses generated by LLMs. The method has been further enhanced by approaches like IRCoT, which interleave retrieval processes with CoT reasoning, leading to significant improvements in retrieval accuracy and reducing model hallucination.
