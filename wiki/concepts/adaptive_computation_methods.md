## Definition
Adaptive computation methods refer to techniques and algorithms designed to optimize computational processes by dynamically adjusting the computational effort based on the complexity of the task at hand. These methods aim to enhance efficiency, speed, and resource utilization, particularly in complex systems such as large-scale machine learning models.

## Context
Adaptive computation methods are crucial in the field of machine learning and artificial intelligence, where models often require significant computational resources. A prominent example is the speculative decoding algorithm, which accelerates inference in large autoregressive models like Transformers. This method enables parallel computation of tokens, thereby speeding up the process without altering the output distribution. Speculative decoding can be integrated into existing models without necessitating retraining or architectural changes, making it highly applicable in production environments.
