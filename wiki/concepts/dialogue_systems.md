## Definition
Dialogue systems are computational systems designed to converse with humans using natural language. These systems can be implemented in various forms, such as chatbots, virtual assistants, or interactive voice response systems, and are used to facilitate human-computer interaction.

## Context
Recent advancements in artificial intelligence, particularly in Large Language Models (LLMs), have significantly enhanced the capabilities of dialogue systems. The introduction of methodologies like Cue-CoT (Chain-of-thought Prompting) has further improved these systems by incorporating intermediate reasoning steps. This approach allows dialogue systems to better personalize and engage responses by identifying linguistic cues related to user status indicators such as personality, emotion, and psychology. The Cue-CoT methodology has been shown to outperform standard prompting methods, particularly in scenarios with limited training data.
