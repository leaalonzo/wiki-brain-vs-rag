## Definition
Subtasks refer to smaller, manageable components of a larger task. In the context of problem-solving and reasoning, breaking down a complex task into subtasks can enhance understanding and improve the accuracy of solutions by allowing for a more focused approach to each component.

## Context
In the realm of large language models (LLMs) and artificial intelligence, subtasks play a crucial role in improving the reasoning capabilities of these models. The "Plan-and-Solve Prompting" method, as introduced in the paper "Plan-and-Solve Prompting: Improving Zero-Shot Chain-of-Thought Reasoning by Large Language Models," leverages the concept of subtasks to enhance zero-shot reasoning. By devising a plan to break down tasks into subtasks, the method addresses common errors such as calculation errors, missing-step errors, and semantic misunderstanding errors, which are prevalent in Zero-shot Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting.
