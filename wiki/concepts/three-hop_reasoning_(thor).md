## Definition
Three-hop Reasoning (THOR) is a framework designed for implicit sentiment analysis (ISA) that employs chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting to simulate human-like reasoning. This method facilitates the identification of implicit aspects, opinions, and sentiment polarities by inferring latent opinions and aspects through a structured, multi-step reasoning process.

## Context
THOR was introduced in the paper "Reasoning Implicit Sentiment with Chain-of-Thought Prompting" as a novel approach to enhance the performance of implicit sentiment analysis. The framework is particularly effective in zero-shot settings, where it significantly improves state-of-the-art performance. THOR leverages a self-consistency mechanism to ensure the accuracy of its reasoning steps and can be adapted to other natural language processing tasks with minimal adjustments.
