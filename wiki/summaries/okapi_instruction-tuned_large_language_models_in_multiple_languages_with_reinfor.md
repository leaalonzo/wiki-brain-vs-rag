## Overview
Okapi is a pioneering system designed to enhance large language models (LLMs) through instruction tuning in multiple languages using reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF). This approach addresses the limitations of existing open-source LLMs, which primarily focus on English and a few other languages, by introducing instruction and response-ranked data in 26 diverse languages. The project aims to improve the accessibility and performance of multilingual LLMs, providing a framework and resources for future research.

## Key Points
- Okapi is the first system to use RLHF for instruction-tuning LLMs in multiple languages.
- The system introduces instruction and response-ranked data in 26 languages.
- Okapi demonstrates the advantages of RLHF over supervised fine-tuning (SFT) for multilingual instruction tuning.
- The project includes benchmark datasets for evaluating generative LLMs in multiple languages.
- Resources and frameworks are released open-source to facilitate further research and development.

## Concepts Introduced
Instruction tuning, large language models (LLMs), reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), supervised fine-tuning (SFT), multilingual instruction tuning
