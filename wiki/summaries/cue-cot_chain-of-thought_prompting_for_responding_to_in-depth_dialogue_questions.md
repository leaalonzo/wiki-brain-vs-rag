## Overview
The paper "Cue-CoT: Chain-of-thought Prompting for Responding to In-depth Dialogue Questions with LLMs" introduces a novel approach to enhance dialogue systems using Large Language Models (LLMs). The proposed method, Cue-CoT, incorporates linguistic cues to improve the inference process, aiming to provide more personalized and engaging responses by considering user personality, emotion, and psychology.

## Key Points
- Cue-CoT introduces an intermediate reasoning step to identify linguistic cues in dialogue, enhancing response generation.
- The approach targets three major linguistic cues: personality, emotion, and psychology.
- A benchmark with six datasets in Chinese and English was created to evaluate the method.
- Experiments show that Cue-CoT outperforms standard prompting methods in terms of helpfulness and acceptability.
- Two variants of Cue-CoT are proposed: O-Cue CoT and M-Cue CoT, with the latter showing superior robustness and reasoning performance.

## Concepts Introduced
Cue-CoT, Large Language Models (LLMs), linguistic cues, chain-of-thought prompting, O-Cue CoT, M-Cue CoT, zero-shot setting, one-shot setting
